The things people ask us most, without the sales pitch.

When are you open?

The emergency line runs 24 hours a day across Kitchener-Waterloo. Emergency calls outside regular hours are billed at a higher rate, and you hear the figure first.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Yes. Every plumber is fully licensed and insured to work in Ontario.

Another plumber left a mess. Can you fix it?

We will assess what was left behind and give you a quote for putting it right.

Do you repair dishwashers and washing machines?

No. We connect the water lines and do the plumbing for appliances, but we do not repair the appliance itself. If it is under warranty, the retailer is the place to start.

Should I use a chemical drain cleaner?

Sparingly, if at all. They can damage pipes and they are hard on the environment. A drain that keeps clogging needs a plumber, not another bottle.

Why is my drain slow?

Almost always a blockage: food waste, congealed oil, something that should not be down there, or tree roots further along the line. We clean and repair drains.

What is that smell from the drain?

Usually the P-trap, the bend that holds water to keep sewer gas out. It may be damaged, or it may never have been fitted. That is a drain repair.

Water is dripping through the ceiling. What do I do?

If it only drips when one tap runs, stop using that tap. If it drips regardless, shut off the water supply. Then call promptly, because waiting raises the cost and can put the structure at risk.

Why is my shower pressure low?

If it is only the shower, the fixture is the likely cause. Taking the showerhead off shows whether it needs cleaning or whether the shower control is at fault.

My toilet rocks. Does it matter?

Yes, and we repair them. A rocking toilet can turn into a leak and then into water damage, so it is worth doing early.

Do you install water heaters?

We repair and install electric hot water heaters, and we fit tankless models.

Is a low-flush toilet worth it?

It is a good choice. It uses less water, which is better for the environment and can bring your water bill down.
